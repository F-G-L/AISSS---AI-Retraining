2. Replace its contents with the contents of the file "evaluate_all_models.py" in the folder "Final" in this repository.
3. Open command prompt.
4. Enter "cd ", followed by the path to your "Cnn_Project_Control" folder.
5. Enter ".venv\Scripts\activate".
6. Enter "python train_Control.py".
7. Wait.
8. Enter "exit".

12. Wait.
